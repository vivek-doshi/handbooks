Step 07 · Intermediate · Vertex AI, Gemini, agents, TPUs, BigQuery ML, and responsible AI on Google Cloud

Google Cloud AI and machine learning

Google Cloud pairs its Gemini models and custom TPU silicon with a unified ML platform and strong data tooling. This step shows how the pieces fit and how to govern them.

2. Models and access

  • Use Gemini through Vertex AI for enterprise controls (IAM, VPC-SC, CMEK, data residency) rather than consumer endpoints.
  • Gemini models are multimodal with long context; choose smaller, faster variants where quality allows. Context caching and batch prediction reduce cost.
  • Grounding with Google Search or your own data reduces hallucination; function calling and structured output connect models to systems.
  • Model Garden hosts open models (Gemma, Llama and others) that you can deploy to your own endpoints.

3. Retrieval

Choose by control needed: Vertex AI Search or RAG Engine for managed pipelines; Vector Search for large-scale, low-latency retrieval you manage; BigQuery or AlloyDB vector features when data already lives there. Preserve document-level access control, and evaluate with the Gen AI evaluation service.

4. Agents

Agent Development Kit (ADK) is the code-first framework; Agent Engine runs agents managed with sessions, memory and tracing; the Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol and MCP support interoperability. Give agents dedicated service accounts, limit tool scope, and add human approval for destructive actions.

5. Custom ML and accelerators

  • Vertex AI Training runs custom and distributed jobs; Pipelines (Kubeflow or TFX based) automate workflows; Model Registry and Model Monitoring support governance.
  • TPUs (Trillium and later generations, plus Ironwood for inference) offer strong price-performance for large models; GPUs give widest compatibility. Use GKE or Vertex for orchestration.
  • Spot VMs and Dynamic Workload Scheduler lower training cost; reservations secure scarce accelerators.

6. Security, safety and cost

  • VPC Service Controls, Private Service Connect, CMEK and Organization Policy restrict data movement and model access.
  • Model Armor screens prompts and responses for injection, data leakage and unsafe content; Gemini safety settings tune filters.
  • Data governance with Dataplex and Sensitive Data Protection (DLP); audit with Cloud Audit Logs.
  • Cost: measure per-feature token usage, use batch and caching, and right-size model choice using evaluation.
PracticeBuild a grounded assistant with Gemini and Vertex AI RAG over documents in Cloud Storage, put it behind IAM with VPC Service Controls, and compare two Gemini variants on quality and cost.
