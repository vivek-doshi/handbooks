Reference stacks by size

Team sizeStackNotes
1 to 5 people, a few modelsGit + DVC + MLflow + GitHub Actions + one managed endpoint or containerAvoid Kubernetes until needed
5 to 30, tens of modelsAdd orchestrator (Dagster or Airflow), feature store if skew hurts, monitoring, shared tracking serverPlatform person part time
30+, hundreds of modelsKubernetes platform: Kubeflow or managed equivalent, KServe, feature store, central registry, GPU scheduling, FinOpsDedicated platform team, self-service templates
Build versus buyBuy or use managed services for undifferentiated plumbing; build only where your constraints (data residency, cost at scale, custom hardware) demand it. A platform nobody uses is the common failure, so treat it as a product with users.

Industry standards and recommendations

  • Maturity and process: Google and Microsoft MLOps maturity models; CRISP-DM style lifecycle; ISO/IEC 5338 (AI system life cycle).
  • Risk and governance: NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system), and the EU AI Act for high-risk systems (documentation, logging, human oversight, post-market monitoring).
  • Documentation: model cards, datasheets for datasets, and system cards.
  • Packaging and interchange: ONNX, safetensors (avoid pickle), OCI images, MLflow model format, OpenAPI for services.
  • Observability: OpenTelemetry (including GenAI conventions), OpenLineage for lineage.
  • Supply chain: SLSA levels, SBOMs, Sigstore signing, and signed model provenance.
  • Security: OWASP LLM Top 10, MITRE ATLAS threat knowledge base.
  • Responsible AI: fairness testing per segment, explainability where required, human-in-the-loop for high-impact decisions.

Recommended practices checklist

AreaPractice
ReproducibilityEvery model traceable to commit, data version, environment and seed
AutomationNo manual production steps; pipelines in version control
TestingData, model and pipeline tests are release gates
ReleaseShadow or canary, automatic rollback
MonitoringDrift, quality and business metrics with owners
GovernanceModel cards, approvals and audit log in the registry
SecuritySigned artifacts, scanned images, least privilege, no pickle from untrusted sources
CostTags, quotas, spot, autoscale to zero, unit cost per prediction
PlatformGolden-path templates, docs and a support channel

Cost and capacity levers

  • Right-size GPUs and use mixed precision; spot with checkpoints for training.
  • Batch scoring over online serving when possible; cache.
  • Share inference GPUs across models; autoscale to zero.
  • Retire unused models and expire old artifacts.
  • See the cloud FinOps handbook.
